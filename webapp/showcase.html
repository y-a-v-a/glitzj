A parrot talks. Is it talking?

Glitzj breaks pictures. Are they broken? Every picture on this page is one photo of two parrots, put through one of ten tools. They look ruined. They are not: the files open in any viewer, and each tool is a real mechanism that did exactly what it was told. It was just told the wrong thing.

Nothing here is a stored image. Your browser is making all of it right now, with the same code as the tools. Point at a strip to see one tool's whole picture, or pick it to read how.

Then break what you broke

Every result can be fed back in as a new source, on the same page or the next one. Three chains of three tools follow. Pick a step to see what it made, and open it in its tool to carry on from there.

So, are they broken?

A parrot repeats the sounds and leaves the meaning out. These tools do the opposite. The sorting algorithm sorted, the television received, the decoder decoded, each to the letter, and none of them was asked whether the picture still made sense. What you see is not damage. It is obedience.
